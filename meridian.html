A TCM Nap Clock

Each two-hour window of the day has a ruling organ-meridian in Traditional Chinese Medicine, and each organ has its own seat in the body.

Meridian fuses that map with Yoga Nidra's practice of nyāsa — rotating awareness through the body — to suggest short restorative micro-naps that either replenish a depleted organ or harmonize one at its peak.

Working windows ask only that you work. Passive windows ask that you sleep. The rest are invitations to lie down for ten or fifteen minutes and let the body be the practice.
